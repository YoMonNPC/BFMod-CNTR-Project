## ⚠️ 風險告知 · Risk Notice

致本項目的使用者：

　　BlockFront（簡稱 BFMod 或 BF）是由 BoehMod / LeScooter 開發的一款二戰題材 Minecraft 模組，目前暫無官方中文譯名，簡體中文社群常用譯名為「方塊前線」，下文亦沿用該譯名。

　　本項目的初衷是方便中文使用者遊玩此模組。自 2023 年以來，截至本文撰寫之日，本項目在各渠道的累計下載量已超過 11,000 次，由衷感謝各位的支援。但遺憾的是，近期出現了一些值得關注的情況：多名使用本項目且實力較強的玩家遭到《方塊前線》官方管理組封鎖；同時，也有玩家因在遊戲內反擊針對中文使用者的歧視性言論，先被管理組禁言，隨後又被追加雲端永久封鎖。由於《方塊前線》允許管理員對使用者遊戲進行螢幕截圖並檢查，結合以上封鎖實例，項目維護者及部分簡體中文玩家合理懷疑該模組的管理組存在對中文使用者的針對性對待。

　　此外，《方塊前線》的隱私政策表明，該模組會收集使用者的硬體資訊以實施「硬體封鎖」，禁止使用者透過更換帳號或裝置來逃避封鎖。這一手段雖能威懾作弊行為，但《方塊前線》並未配備與之匹配的自動且高效的反作弊系統，封鎖基本依賴玩家檢舉與人工審核，主觀性極強；帳號封鎖後即刻解除與 Discord 的關聯，失去訪問頻道的權限，意味著僅憑個人完全無法對封鎖進行申訴。儘管《方塊前線》是免費模組，採用 Microsoft 帳號認證，除贊助外官方不直接從中獲利，但項目維護者依然認為該模組目前的管理方式存在上述不合理之處。

　　以上內容同樣包含個人的主觀判斷。鑑於項目維護者暫無與繁體中文使用者的溝通渠道，上述資訊可能僅適用於簡體中文社群。若您希望規避風險，請謹慎考慮是否繼續使用本項目。若文中所述「針對性對待」的情況得到證實，本項目將考慮停止更新。

    <p>To Users of This Project,</p>
    <p>BlockFront (abbreviated as BFMod or BF) is a World War II-themed Minecraft mod developed by BoehMod / LeScooter.</p>
    <p>This project was created to make the mod more accessible to Chinese-speaking players. Since 2023, as of the date of this notice, the project has accumulated over 11,000 downloads across all distribution channels. I sincerely appreciate everyone's support. Unfortunately, several recent incidents deserve attention. Multiple highly skilled players who used this project have been banned by the official BlockFront moderation team. In addition, some players who responded to discriminatory remarks directed at Chinese users in-game were first muted by the moderators and were later get permanent Cloud ban. Since BlockFront allows administrators to inspect players by taking screenshots during gameplay, and considering these ban cases, I, along with some members of the Simplified Chinese community, have reasonable grounds to suspect that the mod's moderation team may be treating Chinese users unfairly.</p>
    <p>Furthermore, BlockFront's Privacy Policy states that the mod collects users' hardware information in order to enforce hardware bans, preventing banned players from bypassing restrictions by changing accounts or devices. While this approach may discourage cheating, BlockFront does not provide an automated and effective anti-cheat system to complement such a severe punishment mechanism. Instead, bans are largely based on player reports and manual moderation, making the process highly subjective. Once an account is banned, it is immediately unlinked from the official Discord server and loses access to all channels, meaning that individual players are effectively unable to submit an appeal. Although BlockFront is a free mod that uses Microsoft Authentication and does not directly profit from its player base apart from donations, I believes that the current moderation practices remain problematic for the reasons described above.</p>
    <p>The statements above also include my own personal opinions and judgments. As I currently have no reliable means of communicating with the Traditional Chinese community, the situations described here may apply only to the Simplified Chinese community. If you wish to avoid potential risks, please carefully consider whether you want to continue using this project. Should the suspected discriminatory treatment toward Chinese users be substantiated, this project will consider discontinuing future updates.</p>
</details>
